About Me

Clinical mind, engineering hands

Aspiring PhD candidate with a DMD from Azerbaijan Medical University and a Bachelor's in Biomedical Engineering. I bring an interdisciplinary background that integrates clinical dentistry with biomedical innovation — internationally recognized for scientific creativity with 9 invention-exhibition medals and 2 registered patents.

My journey has taken me from designing robotic exoskeletons and brain-signal-driven assistive devices to researching AI-assisted diagnostics and wound healing. I believe interdisciplinary approaches are key to solving complex medical challenges and improving patient outcomes.
